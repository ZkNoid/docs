This section outlines the upcoming milestones for ZkNoid’s two flagship projects: Orbirium and Wizard Battle.

Both products follow parallel development tracks, with Orbirium advancing its idle crafting systems and Wizard Battle progressing toward a fully verifiable zk-powered PvP strategy experience.

## **Orbirium Roadmap**

### **Q3 2025 - Foundations**

✅ V1 game development: crafting tree, resource upgrades, and balancing
✅ Smart contracts deployed on SUI
✅ Leaderboard implementation
✅ State transfer and verification pipeline to Zeko

### **Q4 2025 - Launch & Next Steps**

✅ Orbirium V1  launch on SUI and Zeko
- V2 game design begins:
    - Pets system
    - Wearable items
    - Battle mechanics

### **Q1 2026 - Orbirium V2 Development**

- Full development of the Orbirium V2 systems
- Integration of new gameplay features
